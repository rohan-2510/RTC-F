COACH PHOTO PLACEHOLDER

To add the coach photo:
1. Take or obtain a professional photo of your head coach
2. Rename it to: coach-photo.jpg
3. Place it in this directory (frontend/public/)
4. The photo will automatically appear on the home page

Recommended photo specifications:
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Dimensions: At least 800x800 pixels (square or portrait orientation works best)
- Professional quality with good lighting
- Coach wearing Taekwondo uniform (dobok) with black belt
- Clean background preferred

Alternative: You can use a free stock photo from sites like:
- Unsplash.com
- Pexels.com
- Pixabay.com

Search terms: "taekwondo instructor", "martial arts coach", "taekwondo master"
